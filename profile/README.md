## About Us

**Charged Technology Solutions (CTS)** provides practical, dependable technology services for individuals and organizations. We help clients deploy, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the systems they rely on every day.

From everyday technical support to network and infrastructure projects, our focus is on solutions that are reliable, secure, and built around real-world needs.

## What We Do

- **IT Support & Troubleshooting** — Diagnostics, repairs, maintenance, and end-user assistance.
- **Networking & Connectivity** — Network configuration, upgrades, and performance optimization.
- **Systems Integration** — Connecting hardware, software, and services into cohesive solutions.
- **Infrastructure & Servers** — Deployment, configuration, and ongoing support for business technology.
- **Security & Surveillance** — Technology integration for monitoring and physical-security systems.
